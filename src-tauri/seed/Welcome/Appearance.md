Open **Settings → Appearance**, or press `⌘,`. These settings are saved on this computer, not in the workspace.

## Theme

**System** follows your computer's light or dark mode. **Light** and **Dark** stay put.

## Palette

| Palette    | Feel                |
| ---------- | ------------------- |
| Betelgeuse | Warm ember neutrals |
| Graphite   | Neutral greys       |
| Midnight   | Cool blue slate     |

Every palette has a light and a dark version.

## Accent colour

The accent colours buttons, selections, checkboxes and links: Ember, Blue, Violet, Emerald, Rose or Amber.

## Text size

Small, Default or Large, for the text of every page.

> [!gray] 🖋️
> To change one page instead, use **Style**, **Small text** and **Full width** in its `•••` menu. Those are saved in the page itself. See [[Pages and sub-pages]].
